## Recommended pipeline
1. **Stills:** optional. Omni text-to-video was good enough without Nano Banana first frames.
2. **Live action:** licensed stock first (Pexels), then Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash for the gaps: a 360p test, then one 1080p final at 3–6 s (≈ $0.40–0.80 per shot). Veo 3.1 Preview is retired.
3. **App screens:** Blender headless (`phone_shot.py` / `retexture.py`) with real captures only. Fix the MCP's `BLENDER_PATH` for interactive tweaks.
4. **Voice:** Gemini TTS with an `en-za-*` library voice, at under a cent per ad. A human SA voice artist is still worth it for the hero cut.
5. **Music:** HeyGen catalog (free); Lyria once billing is on, if a custom bed is wanted.
6. **Edit and QA:** HyperFrames (this project). Run check and snapshots, render 9:16 and 1:1, loudnorm to −14 LUFS.
